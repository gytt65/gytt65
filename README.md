## About

I am **C.A. Aniketh**, a software engineering and quantitative finance builder focused on creating practical AI, data, and product systems for markets, research, and decision support.

My work sits at the intersection of **software engineering**, **AI/ML**, **full-stack development**, and **financial systems engineering**. I build applications that translate complex workflows into usable tools: option-pricing engines, volatility research platforms, paper-trading systems, AI-assisted market research terminals, and premium product interfaces.

I care about engineering that is rigorous and explainable: clean data paths, validation gates, model uncertainty, audit trails, user workflows, and product surfaces that help people make better decisions rather than hide complexity.

## Featured Projects

<details open>
<summary><b>OMEGA NOVA — Indian Options Trading Research Platform</b></summary>

<br />

A Python and Streamlit quantitative finance platform for Indian index options, combining live option-chain ingestion, pricing models, volatility surfaces, paper trading, backtesting, and model validation.

| Stack | Scale | Performance | Security | Impact | Repository |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Python, Streamlit, NumPy, SciPy, Pandas, scikit-learn, Plotly | 157 Python files, 93k+ Python LOC, 73 test files, 512 discovered tests | Heston-COS fast pricing, QMC Monte Carlo, cached market-data workflows | Strict live-data mode, source labeling, validation gates, local credential hardening roadmap | Built a serious options decision-support and research system for Nifty market workflows | [OMEGA-LIVE-OPTION-CHAIN-Finale-v10](https://github.com/gytt65/OMEGA-LIVE-OPTION-CHAIN-Finale-v10) |

OMEGA models option pricing as a pipeline rather than a single formula. It includes Black-Scholes baselines, regime-aware NIRV pricing, ML residual correction, SVI/eSSVI/SABR surface logic, Heston jump diffusion, Heston-COS pricing, GEX diagnostics, conformal uncertainty, paper-trading workflows, and historical validation.

</details>
